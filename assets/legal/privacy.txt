WAVR — Privacy & Legal Notice

Wavr is a free, open-source, offline-first music player.
It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data
to external servers owned by its developer.

All data (playlists, track metadata, preferences) is stored
exclusively on your device using local SQLite and SharedPreferences.

Wavr resolves audio streams from third-party platforms
(YouTube, SoundCloud, Audius, Jamendo, etc.). Your use of
those platforms remains subject to their own terms of service.

Wavr does not sell advertising space or user data.
No analytics SDK is included in this application.

Downloaded audio files are stored locally for personal use only.

in your jurisdiction. You are solely responsible for how you
use downloaded content.
